Yepos Manager: delete your account or data

Developer: YEPOS · App: Yepos Manager (Google Play)

Yepos Manager staff accounts are created by the business (merchant) you work for, not inside the app. You can ask for your account or your personal data to be deleted at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Ask your business administrator to remove your staff account in YEPOS. This deletes your sign-in and staff profile.
  2. Or email info@yepos.co.uk with the subject "Yepos Manager deletion request". Include the business name and the email or phone number on your account. We confirm the request with the business and complete it within 30 days.
  3. Customers of a business (for example a reservation or delivery customer) can use the same email address to have their name, phone number, email and address removed.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your staff account, sign-in identifiers, staff profile and any personal contact details we hold for you.
  • Kept: business records the merchant is legally required to keep, such as invoices, orders and payment records, for up to 6 years under UK tax law. Your name is removed from them where the law allows.

Full privacy policy: Yepos Manager Privacy Policy

Yepos Manager：删除账号或数据

Yepos Manager 的员工账号由你所在的商户开通，不在 App 内注册。你可以随时要求删除账号或个人数据。

如何申请

  1. 请商户管理员在 YEPOS 中删除你的员工账号，登录信息与员工资料随之删除。
  2. 或发邮件到 info@yepos.co.uk，主题写「Yepos Manager deletion request」，注明商户名称和账号上的邮箱或电话。我们与商户确认后在 30 天内完成。
  3. 商户的顾客（如预订、外送顾客）也可以通过同一邮箱要求删除姓名、电话、邮箱和地址。

删除什么、保留什么

  • 删除：员工账号、登录标识、员工资料，以及我们保存的你的个人联系方式。
  • 保留：商户依法必须保存的经营记录（发票、订单、付款记录），按英国税法最长保留 6 年；在法律允许的范围内去除其中的姓名。
