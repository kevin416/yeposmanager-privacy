Yepos Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Yepos Manager is a business application for authorised staff of restaurants, takeaways, tea shops and wholesalers that use YEPOS services. Staff accounts are created by the merchant or its administrator; accounts cannot be created inside the app. The merchant is responsible for the business records it keeps in YEPOS; YEPOS processes them on the merchant's behalf.

Information we process

  • Sign-in and account information: staff account identifier, password during sign-in, authentication token, staff profile, company membership and permissions. If you turn on biometric sign-in, your fingerprint or face data never leaves the device; the app only receives a yes/no result from Android.
  • Business operations: tables, orders, products, prices, stock, payments, expenses, receivables, staff timetables and reports needed to run the business.
  • Customer records kept by the merchant: names, phone numbers, email addresses and delivery addresses entered for reservations, takeaway and delivery orders, members, loyalty, top-ups and gift vouchers.
  • Device and printer settings: receipt and kitchen printer details (IP address, port, Bluetooth address) and app preferences, stored on the device.

Camera, photos and Bluetooth

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to photograph receipts or products. Barcode images are processed on the device. A photo is uploaded only when the user chooses to attach it to a product or an expense. Bluetooth is used only to find and connect to receipt printers; Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning on older versions, but the app does not collect or store your location.

Receipt text recognition (expenses)

When a user scans a receipt while adding an expense, that receipt image is sent to Baidu AI Cloud's text-recognition (OCR) service to read the amount, date and supplier. Only the receipt image is sent; no account or customer data is attached. The recognised text is returned to the app and the user reviews it before saving.

Where information goes

  • YEPOS servers, to store and synchronise the merchant's business records across its devices and to deliver real-time updates such as new online orders.
  • Baidu AI Cloud, only for receipt images as described above.
  • Printers on the merchant's own network or paired over Bluetooth.

All network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Business records are retained according to the merchant's operational, contractual and legal requirements. Staff who want their account removed, and customers who want their details deleted, should contact the merchant administrator first. You can also contact YEPOS directly using the details below and we will act on the request together with the merchant.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact YEPOS at info@yepos.co.uk or call +44 7550 006600.

Yepos Manager 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

Yepos Manager 是供使用 YEPOS 服务的餐厅、外卖店、奶茶店和批发商的授权员工使用的业务 App。员工账号由商户或其管理员开通，App 内不能注册账号。商户对其存放在 YEPOS 中的经营记录负责，YEPOS 代商户处理这些数据。

我们处理的信息

  • 登录与账号信息：员工账号、登录时输入的密码、登录令牌、员工资料、所属公司和权限。开启生物识别登录时，指纹或面部数据不会离开设备，App 只从系统拿到「是否通过」的结果。
  • 经营数据：桌台、订单、产品、价格、库存、付款、支出、应收、员工排班和报表等经营所需记录。
  • 商户保存的顾客记录：预订、外卖与外送订单、会员、积分、充值和礼品券中录入的姓名、电话、邮箱和送货地址。
  • 设备与打印机设置：小票/厨房打印机信息（IP 地址、端口、蓝牙地址）和 App 偏好，保存在设备上。

相机、照片与蓝牙

相机用于扫描条码，以及给小票或产品拍照。条码图像在设备上处理；照片只有在用户选择附到产品或支出上时才会上传。蓝牙只用于查找并连接小票打印机；旧版 Android 做蓝牙扫描需要定位权限，但 App 不收集也不保存你的位置。

小票文字识别（支出）

录入支出时如果扫描小票，该小票图片会发送到百度智能云的文字识别（OCR）服务，用于读出金额、日期和供应商。只发送小票图片，不附带任何账号或顾客数据；识别结果返回 App，由用户核对后再保存。

信息去向

  • YEPOS 服务器：存储并在商户的各台设备之间同步经营记录，并推送新网单等实时更新。
  • 百度智能云：仅限上述小票图片。
  • 商户自己网络里的打印机，或经蓝牙配对的打印机。

所有网络传输均加密（HTTPS/TLS）。我们不出售个人信息，不用于广告；App 不含任何广告或统计 SDK。

保留与删除

经营记录按商户的经营、合同和法律要求保留。员工要注销账号、顾客要删除自己的信息，请先联系商户管理员；也可以按下方方式直接联系 YEPOS，我们会与商户一起处理。
